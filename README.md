# Kids Daily ⭐

A simple daily points app for Diyar, Dayan and Dilan. It is a single `index.html` file with no build step.

**Open it:** https://bornaxahadi.github.io/kids-daily/

## How the kids use it

- On the home screen, tap your own card.
- Tap a green **Good things** tile when you do something good (homework, cleaning up, reading, playing outside…) to earn points.
- Red **Oops things** tiles take points away (fighting, leaving a mess, too much YouTube…).
- Every tap waits for Dad to check it (⏳). Points only count after Dad confirms.
- Reach the daily target (12 points by default) to win 40 Robux the next morning.
- Use the **Roblox time** panel: tap **Start playing** when you begin and **Stop playing** when you finish. The timer counts your daily limit (2 hours normally, 3 hours on special days). Going over the limit costs points.
- The date and weekday are shown big at the top. Use ◀ ▶ to look back at earlier days.
- When points change, the score counts up (green ▲) or down (red ▼).
- **Girl of the Week:** most points Monday to Sunday wins 50 AED. On a tie, the tied kids split it. **Progress** shows week and month charts, a strong/weak breakdown per kid (study, tidying, behavior, outside, screen time), what each kid does most, and how they compare with the week or month before.

## Look, sounds and TV

- Blocky game-style look with an animated logo and a blocky character for each kid that waves, jumps when they earn points and looks sad on an oops.
- Game sound effects for good taps, oops taps, confirms, level-ups, the target fanfare and the Roblox timer (start, stop, time's up). Turn them on or off with the 🔊 / 🔇 button.
- **TV:** open the website in the TV browser. Use the remote's arrow keys to move between buttons, OK/Enter to press, and Back to return home. Works in landscape on big screens.

## For parents

- Tap **✅ Check** on the home screen and enter the PIN to review what the kids tapped today.
  - **✅ Confirm**: the kid gets the points and **+1 trust point 💛**.
  - **❌ Deny**: removes it, no points.
  - **Change…**: swap it to the right activity; those points count (no trust point).
  - **Confirm all** approves everything waiting for one kid.
- **🌟 Bonus points** (Parent): give +1, +2, +3 or +5 for extra-good things, with an optional reason. They count right away.
- **Fix mistakes** (Parent): change or remove any entry, on any day, with the PIN.
- Kids also earn levels (🌱 Seedling up to 👑 Legend) from confirmed points, and a 🔥 streak for days in a row at the target.

- Tap **Parent** and enter the PIN. **The default PIN is `1234` — change it right away** in the PIN section.
- In Parent mode you can set the daily target, Roblox limits, mark special days, set Star prizes, mark Robux as given, and remove wrong taps (Remove buttons appear on each kid's page for 10 minutes).

## Where the points are saved

On this GitHub Pages site, points and settings are saved **in the browser on each device only** (localStorage). A tablet and a phone will each keep their own separate scores, and clearing browser data erases them.

The original Claude artifact link syncs points across all devices. Use that link if the kids share more than one device.
